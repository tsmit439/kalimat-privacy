Kalimát privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Kalimát is a free word game that uncovers a quote from the Bahá'í writings each day. It is made by Tristan Smith as a non-commercial project. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it is suitable for all ages.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (which days you've played, your guesses and your streak) and your settings are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we receive, and only if you choose to send it

The app has a Share feedback form in Settings and a Report an issue button under each game. If you use one and tap Send, we receive:

  • the message you typed (optional for issue reports)
  • for issue reports: the day's quote, which game you were playing, and your answers in that game so far, so we can see exactly what went wrong
  • the app version, whether you use iPhone or Android, and your screen size

We don't receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any device identifier, and we don't link reports to you. Please don't put personal details in your message.

We use these reports only to fix problems and improve Kalimát. They are stored with our database provider, Supabase (supabase.com), in Sydney, Australia, sent over an encrypted connection, and readable only by the developer. Like most online services, Supabase may keep short-lived technical logs, such as the network address a request came from, to keep its service running securely. We delete reports when we no longer need them, and at most 2 years after they're sent.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics companies in the app
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of data
  • No accounts or sign-in

Children

Kalimát is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can use Kalimát fully without ever sending feedback. If you sent a report and want it deleted, email info@tristansmith.com.au with roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we'll remove it.

The quotes

The quotes are from The Hidden Words of Bahá'u'lláh, translated by Shoghi Effendi, from the Bahá'í Reference Library. Copyright © Bahá'í International Community.

Changes and contact
